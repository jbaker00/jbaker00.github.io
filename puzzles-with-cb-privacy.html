Privacy Policy

Puzzles with CB · Last updated 24 July 2026

Puzzles with CB does not ask you to create an account, and the developer never sees your name, email address or photos. The app has no login and stores nothing about you on any server we run.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Your progress on a puzzle lives only on your device for as long as you are playing it. There is no account, no cloud sync and no server belonging to the developer.

Advertising

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may receive your device's advertising identifier, general device information such as model and operating system version, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.

Before any of that happens, iOS asks whether you allow tracking. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you will see less relevant ads instead. Nothing else about the app changes. If you are in the UK or EEA you will also be shown a consent form covering personalised advertising.

Google's handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms.

Analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count anonymous, aggregated events — for example how many puzzles are started, or which piece counts are popular. This tells the developer which parts of the app are actually used. It is not linked to your identity and is never sold.

Photos

If you tap Screenshot my puzzle and choose to save the picture, the app asks permission to add that one image to your photo library. It only ever adds an image. The app cannot read, browse or upload anything already in your library.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no chat, no user accounts and no way for players to contact each other.

Your choices

  • Change tracking permission any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset the advertising identifier in the same place.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
