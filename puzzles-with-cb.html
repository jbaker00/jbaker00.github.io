Every puzzle here is a real jigsaw

Each one was assembled by hand on a kitchen table, photographed once it was finished, and straightened out so only the artwork remains. The pictures you are piecing together are the same ones somebody already spent an evening putting together for real.

How it plays

🧩

Proper Jigsaw Pieces

Interlocking tabs and sockets, cut fresh from the artwork each time you play. Drag a piece near where it belongs and it snaps into place with a satisfying little tap.

🔢

12 to 300 Pieces

The suggested count keeps pieces a comfortable size for your device, so nothing is ever too fiddly to grab.

🔍

Peek at the Picture

Forgot what you are aiming for? Tap the small picture at the top for a closer look, then carry straight on.

✨

Finish It

Stuck, or just curious how it ends? Watch the pieces walk themselves home one at a time. Speed it up, or hit Stop and take over — everything already placed stays put.

💾

Save Your Finish

Finished a puzzle? Save a picture of it to share.

🌙

No Pressure

No account, no sign-up, no timer, and nothing to lose if you put it down halfway. Just a quiet puzzle whenever you want one.

Three Ways to Play

Easy — the finished picture shows faintly on the board to guide you Medium — the same guide, but every piece starts turned Advanced — a blank board and turned pieces, no help at all
