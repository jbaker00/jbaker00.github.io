DogTalk

What is your dog saying? Tap, let your dog bark, and find out what they really meant.

Download on the App Store

“Walk? Walk. Walk! WALK.”  ·  “Squirrel! Squirrel! SQUIRREL!”

Tap, bark, translate. Hit the big button and let your dog talk for up to 15 seconds. DogTalk listens to how loud, how long, and how high-pitched the sound was, then picks the perfect line for the moment.

What you get

Four kinds of dog

Barks, growls, whines, and howls are each detected from the sound itself.

A translation for every sound

Each one comes with a mood and a (very scientific) confidence score.

Session log

Scroll back through the whole conversation with your dog.

One-tap sharing

Send your dog's best quotes to friends and family.

Private by design. Audio is analyzed live on your device and never saved or uploaded. Only a few numbers (loudness and pitch) exist during analysis, and they're discarded immediately. Translations vanish when you close the app.

Just for fun. Dogs don't actually speak English. The translations and confidence scores are playful guesses based on the sound alone — DogTalk is for entertainment, not a substitute for knowing your dog. If your dog won't cooperate, feel free to bark yourself.
