Using DogTalk

How does it work?

Tap the big button, let your dog bark, whine, growl, or howl (up to 15 seconds), then tap again or wait for it to stop on its own. DogTalk measures how loud, how long, and how high-pitched the sound was, classifies it, and picks a fitting "translation."

Is this a real translation?

No — it's for entertainment. Dogs don't speak English. The translations, moods, and confidence percentages are playful guesses, not a behavioral or veterinary tool.

My dog won't bark on command.

Totally normal. Try it when they're excited (doorbell, walk time, squirrel), or demonstrate a bark yourself — the app doesn't judge.

It says "Quiet" every time.

The sound wasn't loud enough above the room's background noise. Get the phone closer to your dog, and make sure nothing is covering the microphone at the bottom of the phone.

Microphone & Privacy

I see "Can't Listen" or the app asks for microphone access.

DogTalk needs the microphone to hear your dog. If you declined the prompt, enable it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → DogTalk.

Is my audio recorded or uploaded?

No. Audio is analyzed live on your device, never saved, and never sent anywhere. The app has no accounts; it shows ads and collects anonymous usage analytics to stay free — see the privacy policy.

Where did my earlier translations go?

Translations are kept only for the current session and vanish when you close the app. Use "Share what they said" to save one somewhere.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep DogTalk free. When iOS asks for tracking permission you can choose whether ads are personalized — declining still shows ads, just non-personalized ones.
