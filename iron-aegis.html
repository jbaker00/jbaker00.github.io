Iron Aegis

Enemy warheads are raining down on your cities. Tap to fire — protect all 6 before they're gone.

Download on the App Store

Classic arcade city defense. You control ground-based missile batteries — tap anywhere to fire an interceptor, and time your shots so the blast radius catches incoming warheads before they land.

What it does

Tap to fire

Tap where you want to fire and your nearest battery launches automatically. No aiming reticle to fuss with.

3 batteries, 6 cities

Defend all 6 cities with three missile batteries. Lose all six and the game's over.

Endless waves

Each wave comes faster and heavier than the last — how long can your cities survive?

Bonus scoring

Surviving cities and unused ammo pay off at the end of every wave.

Pause anytime

Step away mid-wave and pick up right where you left off.

Smooth 60fps

SpriteKit graphics tuned for iPhone and iPad.

Free to play, supported by ads.
