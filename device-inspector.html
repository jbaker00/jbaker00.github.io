Device Inspector

A complete, real-time view of your iPhone or iPad's hidden configuration — the same data ad networks and analytics SDKs read every time you open an app.

Download on the App Store

Built for debugging attribution. If you work in mobile advertising, analytics, or app development, Device Inspector shows you precisely what iOS exposes — no guessing.

What it shows

IDFA & ATT status

Your advertising identifier plus its current App Tracking Transparency status — Authorized, Denied, Restricted, or Not Determined.

Device details

Model, OS version, build number, kernel info, screen metrics, and processor/memory stats.

Network & carrier

Wi-Fi, cellular, and VPN interface details, plus carrier name, MCC/MNC, and radio access technology.

Locale & permissions

Locale, region, languages, timezone, push notification status, battery, and thermal state.

Copy any value

Tap any row to copy it to the clipboard, or use the search bar to filter across every field instantly.

Private by design

Every value shown stays on your device — none of it is ever collected. The only network activity is loading the AdMob banner ad and anonymous usage analytics (never the values themselves).

IDFA reads as all-zeros until App Tracking Transparency is granted — and always on the Simulator, use a real device.
