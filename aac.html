My Words

An AAC app that turns an iPhone or iPad into a voice output communication aid — for non-verbal individuals, and the families and therapists who support them.

Download on the App Store

Tap a picture, hear a voice. My Words is built for people with autism, apraxia, cerebral palsy, or any condition that makes spoken communication difficult — large, colorful buttons speak the word or phrase out loud the moment you tap them.

What it does

Natural AI voices

Choose from multiple AI text-to-speech voices across two providers, or use the built-in iOS voice completely offline.

Fully customizable

Add buttons with any photo from your camera roll, edit labels, spoken phrases, and colors, and reorder buttons to put the most-used ones first.

Built for accessibility

Extra-large buttons, high-contrast borders, and visual feedback while speaking — easy to see and tap even with fine motor challenges.

iPhone & iPad

Works in portrait or landscape, on either device.

Privacy first

No personal data is collected, stored, or shared. Photos stay on the device; voice requests are processed securely.

Free · iPhone & iPad.
