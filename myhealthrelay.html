MyHealthRelay

From your Apple Health data to a report your doctor can actually read. Pick a time range, tap Generate, and share a clean PDF in seconds.

Download on the App Store

Built for the appointment, not the archive. Apple Health quietly collects months of weight, steps, sleep, and heart data — MyHealthRelay turns it into a report you can actually hand your care team.

What it does

Reads Apple Health

Weight, body fat, steps, sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, blood pressure and more — pulled straight from the Health data already on your phone.

One-tap PDF reports

Pick 1W, 1M, 3M, 1Y, All, or a custom range, preview it, then share via AirDrop, Mail, Messages, or Files.

Customizable dashboard

Drag to reorder tiles or hide the ones you don't need. Your layout is saved across launches.

Imports Fitbit & Google data

Bring in a Google Takeout export and MyHealthRelay writes your weight, body fat, steps, and sleep straight into Apple Health.

Fast long-range summaries

Smart aggregates keep 1-year and All-time reports quick, so you're not waiting on months of raw samples.

Private by design

Your Health data stays in Apple Health and on your device — it's never shared with the ad SDK.

Free, with one small ad. A banner ad at the bottom of the dashboard keeps the app free. A Pro upgrade with extra report templates and chart styles is on the way — everything that works today stays free.

Requires iOS 17 or later.
Works with Apple Watch, Wi-Fi scales, and connected blood-pressure cuffs · light & dark mode.
