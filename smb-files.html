SMB Files

The network shares already on your NAS, PC, or Mac — open on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Coming soon to the App Store

The shares you already have, without the setup. If a machine on your network shares files over SMB — a Synology or QNAP NAS, a Windows PC, a Mac with File Sharing on, a Samba box — SMB Files signs in with the same username and password you already use and gets straight to your folders.

What it does

Finds your shares

Sign in once and every share your account can reach is listed, ready to open.

Browse fast

Folders first, instant navigation, and file sizes and dates at a glance.

Move files both ways

Download to your device and upload back to the share, plus rename, delete, and new folders.

Connection info

See the server, share, and a live log of every operation the app ran — the fastest way to work out what a stubborn server is doing.

Private by design
