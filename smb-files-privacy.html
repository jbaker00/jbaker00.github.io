Privacy Policy

SMB Files · Last updated July 2026

Your files and credentials stay between your device and your server. SMB Files connects directly to the machines on your network — your file names, contents, and passwords never pass through us or any cloud service.

Servers and credentials

The servers you add (address, port, username, and options) are stored only on your device. Your passwords are stored in the device Keychain, protected by the operating system, and are used solely to sign in to the servers you configured. We never receive, store, or have access to any of them.

Connections are made directly from your device to the server you name — typically a NAS, a Windows PC, or a Mac on your own network. We operate no servers that see your data, and there is no account system.

Files

Files you browse, download, upload, rename, or delete move directly between your device and your SMB server. Files you download are stored on your device; nothing is ever uploaded to us.

Local network access

Because SMB servers usually live on your local network, iOS asks for permission to find and connect to devices on it. This permission is used only to reach the server address you entered. The app does not scan, catalogue, or report the other devices on your network.

Connection info

The in-app connection view shows diagnostics about your own session — the server you are connected to, the share, and a log of recent operations. This information is generated and displayed on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Advertising (iPhone & iPad)

SMB Files is free and supported by ads provided through Google AdMob on iOS. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use:

  • Device identifiers and advertising data — used to deliver and measure ads, including personalized ads where you have consented
  • Product interaction and diagnostics — used for ad delivery, analytics, and app performance
  • Coarse location — where enabled, to provide regionally relevant ads

On first launch we ask for your consent (via Google's consent form and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) before any personalized ads are shown. You can change tracking permission anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Remove ads

The optional one-time SMB Files Pro purchase removes all ads. Purchases are processed by Apple; we do not receive or store your payment information.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or browsing history. We do not log your servers, credentials, or files, and we operate no servers that receive your data.

Children

SMB Files is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. It is a utility for accessing your own servers.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
